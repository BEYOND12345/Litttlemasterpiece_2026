Upload Mockup Templates to Cloudinary

This tool will help you upload your mockup template images to Cloudinary and update the database.

Instructions:

  1. Select a mockup image file from your computer
  2. Choose the product type
  3. Configure where the artwork should appear (width, height, position)
  4. Click "Upload to Cloudinary"
  5. The template will be added to your database automatically
